I added another small Mandarin study tool to the site: Tone Garden (lol, not a fan of the name, tbh), a browser game for practicing the tones of simplified Chinese words.

The game shows a word and asks for its pinyin or tone numbers. For example, for 白色, you can enter `bái sè`, `bai2 se4`, or simply `24`. After each answer, it shows the pronunciation, English meaning, and an example sentence, with audio when a Mandarin browser voice is available.

There is also a character review mode where you can choose a syllable like `shi` and compare characters across tones, then listen to their source words for context.
